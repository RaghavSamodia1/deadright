DeadRight 🔥

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 July 2026

The short version. DeadRight tracks informal bets between friends. No real money is ever involved. We collect the least we can get away with, we don't sell it, there are no ad networks or trackers in the app, and you can delete everything from inside Settings.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes — we never see them.
Display name and handleSo friends can find you and see who called what.
Profile photo (optional)Only if you choose one.
Your bets, sides, groups, Cookie Jar entries and ledgerThis is the product. It is shared with the members of the groups you join.
Photos you upload as dispute evidenceVisible only to members of that bet's group.
Notifications we've sent youTo show your alerts list and mark them read.

We do not collect your contacts, your location, your device identifiers for advertising, or your browsing activity. There is no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK in the app.

Party Pools

Anyone can join a Party Pool from a shared link without an account. In that case the only thing stored is the display name they type and the option they pick. No email, no account, no cookies for tracking. Everyone with the link can see the pool's results.

Who else sees it

  • Other users: the people in your groups see the bets, jar entries and ledger amounts for those groups. Your handle and Cred score are visible to people you share a group with.
  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage. They process this data on our behalf.
  • Anthropic — only if you use the optional "sharpen this call" suggestion. The text of that one statement is sent to their API to be rewritten more precisely. Nothing else about you is sent, and the feature is skipped entirely if it isn't configured.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising or cross-context behavioural profiling.

No real money

DeadRight is a scorekeeper. Stakes — "a tenner", "a round", "bragging rights" — are recorded as a ledger between friends and settled by you, in person, however you like. We hold no funds, process no payments, and take no cut. There is no payment processor connected to this app, so we never see card or bank details.

Keeping it

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account and we remove your profile, bets you created, your jar entries, your ledger rows and your uploaded evidence. Two things survive by necessity: entries in other people's bets are anonymised rather than deleted, so their history stays intact, and we may retain minimal records where the law requires.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and irreversible.
  • Correct your details: Settings → Profile.
  • Turn off notifications: Settings → Notifications.
  • Block someone: Settings → Blocked users.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we'll action it.

Children

DeadRight is not intended for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll remove it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to group data is enforced in the database itself with row-level security, so one group cannot read another's bets. No system is perfect; if we ever have a breach affecting you, we'll tell you.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we'll update the date above and let you know in the app before it takes effect.
