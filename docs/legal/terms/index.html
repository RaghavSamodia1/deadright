1. What this is

DeadRight is a record-keeping app. You and your friends write down what you called, who took which side, and who turned out to be right. The app tracks a reputation score ("Cred") based on your history.

DeadRight does not accept, hold, transfer, escrow or pay out money or anything of monetary value. Stakes recorded in the app — including amounts shown in the ledger or Cookie Jar — are a note to yourselves about a private arrangement between friends. Every figure in the app is bookkeeping only.

We are not a party to your arrangements and we will not enforce them. If someone doesn't pay up, that is between you. You are responsible for making sure whatever you agree is lawful where you live.
